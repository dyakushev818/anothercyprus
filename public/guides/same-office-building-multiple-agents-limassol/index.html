The Same Limassol Office Building Listed by Several Agents: How to Compare Offers

Seeing the same commercial building on several websites can make a purchase harder to assess. Different photographs, area figures or descriptions may leave you wondering whether the offers are really comparable. The useful next step is to build one consistent fact sheet for the building and compare each proposal against it.

Confirm that every listing describes the same asset

Match the location, exterior, floor arrangement and project name. Request a property reference and current plans from the person handling your enquiry. Establish whether the offer covers the whole building, selected floors or a different ownership interest.

For Germasogeia Corporate Prime, the Another Cyprus listing presents a whole completed commercial office building in Potamos Germasogeias, Limassol. Use that scope as the starting point when comparing another proposal, then verify it in the written offer and transaction documents.

Put area figures on the same measurement basis

Two advertisements can display different square-metre figures because they describe different components. Ask each contact to distinguish internal office space, common areas, covered verandas, uncovered areas, parking and roof space. A smaller headline figure does not by itself establish a different building or a better deal.

The Another Cyprus listing states 1,934 m² gross total area. Ask for the current breakdown and the measurement basis before calculating or comparing a price per square metre. For a company headquarters, usable workspace also matters; for any proposed letting, establish the space actually available for that use.

  • Compare the same floor-by-floor drawings and revision date.
  • Record gross, covered and usable areas separately where supplied.
  • Identify which external and common areas are included in each figure.
  • Resolve material differences in writing before negotiating.

Compare written terms rather than headline prices

Record the price, whether VAT is included or additional, the proposed purchase scope, included equipment, parking allocation, any buyer fees and the validity date of the offer. Ask who is authorised to confirm those terms. Differences should be explained before you treat one advertisement as cheaper.

The published Another Cyprus asking price is €8.7 million plus VAT. This is a marketing reference that requires current written confirmation. Do not infer a particular tax outcome from wording in an advertisement; transaction-specific questions belong with your independent advisers.

Check completion and included items using current evidence

An older advertisement may describe a construction stage while another page shows a completed building. Request dated photographs and arrange a viewing. Confirm what is installed and what is included in the proposed handover, rather than relying on a mix of renders and actual images.

The Another Cyprus page describes Germasogeia Corporate Prime as completed and uses actual building photographs. Its stated parking, EV charging and roof-garden features should still be checked against the current plans, specification and physical inspection.

Choose the contact who can organise a clear information trail

Keep a record of the plans received, viewing arrangements, outstanding questions and written responses. A useful contact can explain the source and date of the information, arrange access and obtain answers from the relevant parties. The number of websites displaying a building does not establish the quality of that process.
