Buying an Office Building in Limassol for Your Company Headquarters

When a company buys its own office building, the first question is whether the space will work for the business. An impressive exterior is useful, but the decision also depends on the floor plans, staff journeys, fit-out and the time needed to become operational. Here is how to turn a property viewing into a useful headquarters assessment.

Write the business brief before comparing buildings

List the teams who will occupy the building, their working patterns and the spaces they share. Reception, meeting rooms, quiet rooms, IT equipment, storage and staff facilities need room as well as desks. Describe both the initial occupation and a realistic expansion scenario.

A whole building can offer more control over the workplace, but also more responsibility for its operation. Decide who will manage maintenance and suppliers, and whether any unused floors would remain available for expansion or be considered for letting.

Test the floor plans against actual furniture and circulation

Ask for dimensioned plans and a floor-by-floor area schedule. Have a workplace designer place desks, meeting rooms and circulation routes on those plans before estimating capacity. Gross area alone does not establish how many people can work comfortably in a building.

The advertised gross total for Germasogeia Corporate Prime is 1,934 m². Its listing separately identifies covered space and external areas. Use the current area schedule to establish which spaces suit your proposed occupation; do not treat the gross total as usable office area.

  • Identify the area available for workstations and meeting rooms.
  • Check the route between reception, lifts, floors and shared facilities.
  • Discuss accessibility, intended use and occupancy requirements with your technical advisers.
  • Confirm what the existing specification includes and what your fit-out must add.

Make parking and arrival part of the viewing

The listing states 18 parking spaces and four EV charging points. Compare those figures with your staff, visitors and delivery needs rather than assuming they are sufficient. During a viewing, inspect the spaces, access and the route into the building.

Visit the location at the times your team would normally arrive and leave. Test the actual commute and nearby services. The stated distance of approximately 400 metres from the coast describes the location; it does not answer how the workplace will operate on a busy weekday.

Separate a completed building from a ready workplace

Germasogeia Corporate Prime is marketed as completed, with actual photographs on the property page. That makes a physical inspection possible. It does not establish that your company can move in immediately without further work.

Prepare a schedule for the building inspection, document review, interior fit-out, furniture, IT installation and commissioning. Ask for the installed-systems specification and handover records, then establish what your own team and contractors must deliver before occupation.

Use a single written brief to request the next step

Send your expected team size, required occupation date, parking needs and preferred viewing dates. Request the current floor plans, area schedule, inclusion specification and written purchase terms.
