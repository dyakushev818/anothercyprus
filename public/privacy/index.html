Privacy and analytics

Another Cyprus uses Google Consent Mode. Before a visitor chooses, Google may receive limited anonymous measurement signals without analytics cookies. Full Google Analytics storage and reporting start only after a visitor gives consent. Declining does not limit access to the website or property information.

Information collected

With consent, Google Analytics may process page views, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source, campaign parameters, scrolling and outbound-link interactions. Without consent, only cookieless consent-mode measurement signals may be sent. Property enquiry events identify the property and contact channel but must not transmit the visitor’s name, phone number, email or message to Google Analytics.

Purpose and legal basis

Analytics helps us measure property-page performance, understand marketing sources and improve the website. Full analytics storage is based on consent.

Lead enquiries

When a visitor chooses to contact us through WhatsApp, telephone or email, the information they send is processed to answer the property enquiry and arrange follow-up. WhatsApp and Google process information under their own privacy terms.

Retention and choices

The browser stores the analytics choice locally. Visitors can clear this site’s browser storage to make a new choice. Analytics information is aggregated for reporting and is not sold by Another Cyprus.
